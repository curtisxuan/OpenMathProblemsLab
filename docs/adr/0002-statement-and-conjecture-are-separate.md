# Statement and Conjecture are separate entities

A Statement is one verbatim occurrence of an open problem in one Paper; a Conjecture is the canonical claim that one or more Statements express. We keep them apart so that provenance stays exact and judgment stays singular: a Statement can always be traced to the sentence it came from, and a Conjecture is assessed once no matter how many Papers mention it.

## Considered Options

Recording occurrences only was simpler and carried no merge risk, but leaves the same claim scattered across records with nowhere to attach a judgment. Writing straight into canonical records was more compact, but destroys the verbatim text and makes a bad merge unrecoverable — with no human reviewing at volume, an unrecoverable silent merge is the worse failure.
